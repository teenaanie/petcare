Pippy holds your pet's whole health record, and tells you the morning something is due — so you don't have to carry it in your head.

Built for the person who looks after the animals. One app for every pet in the house, whatever they are.

WHAT PIPPY KEEPS

• Vaccinations — every shot and booster with its date, and what's due next
• Medicines — doses, how often, and when the next one falls
• Vet visits — diagnoses, treatments, and what was said
• Allergies and ongoing conditions
• Weight, tracked over time
• Bills, so you know what a year of care actually costs
• Photos, for the things easier shown than written

REMINDERS THAT ARRIVE

A booster due next month is no use to anyone if you only find out next year. Pippy emails you the morning something is due, and shows it on the app's own bell. Nothing is added behind your back — you choose what gets a reminder.

LESS TYPING

Photograph a vet report or a vaccination card and Pippy reads it, then shows you what it found so you can correct it before anything is saved. Or just talk — say what happened on the way home from the clinic and it turns into proper records you can check.

Everything the app reads or hears is yours to edit first. It proposes; you decide.

FINDING HELP NEARBY

A directory of vets, groomers, boarders, trainers and pet shops across Pune and Kerala, with more being added. Keep the ones you use, so their details are there when you need them at 2am.

SHARING, WHEN SOMEONE ELSE HAS YOUR PET

Going away? Share a pet with a sitter or boarder so they can see the allergies, the medicines and the vet's number — without being able to change anything.

AN EMERGENCY CARD

The things a stranger would need to know, on one screen, for the day you can't explain them yourself.

FOR BOARDING AND GROOMING BUSINESSES

There's a side of Pippy for providers too: who is with you now, who is coming, and what needs attention before an animal arrives. It works whether or not your customers use the app.

WHAT PIPPY IS NOT

Pippy is a record keeper, not a vet. It doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't replace anyone who went to veterinary school. When something seems wrong with your animal, ring your vet.

PRIVACY

Your pets' records are yours. They are not sold, and they are not shared with anyone you haven't shared them with. You can read what's kept and delete your account, with everything in it, from inside the app.
